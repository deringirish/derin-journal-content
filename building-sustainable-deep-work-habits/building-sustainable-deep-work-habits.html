3. Focus Habits & Rules

  • Recommended Best Practice: Block out 3 consecutive hours every morning with zero notifications enabled.
  • Recommended Best Practice: Write down your top 1 priority task the evening before.
  • Anti-Pattern: Don't check messaging apps during deep coding sessions.
